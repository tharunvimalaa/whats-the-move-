# Reason codes

Codes appear in `options[].reason_codes`, plan `provisional_reasons`, `academic_coverage[].reason_codes`, `warnings`, `questions` and `errors`. Translate them into everyday words for the student.

## Input errors (`status: invalid_input`)
`INVALID_TIMEZONE`, `INVALID_PLANNING_DATE`, `INVALID_WINDOW`, `WINDOW_DATE_MISMATCH`, `INVALID_BUDGET`, `INVALID_DATETIME`, `INVALID_TIME_RANGE` (a commitment ends before it starts), `INVALID_TRAVEL_LEG`, `INVALID_EFFORT`, `DUPLICATE_ID`.

## Warnings and questions
`CALENDAR_UNAVAILABLE`, `CANVAS_NOT_CONNECTED`, `CANVAS_DATA_STALE`, `CANVAS_SYNC_TIME_UNKNOWN`, `CANDIDATE_CAP_APPLIED`, `AS_OF_DEFAULTED`, `SYNTHETIC_DATA`, `COMMITMENT_OVERLAP`, `COMMITMENT_OUTSIDE_WINDOW`, `START_LOCATION_UNKNOWN`, `QUESTION_COMMITMENTS`, `QUESTION_TASK_STATUS`, `QUESTION_TASK_DUE`, `QUESTION_EFFORT`, `QUESTION_READY_TO_SUBMIT`, `QUESTION_VERIFY_OPTION`.

## Academic
`ACADEMIC_SUBMITTED`, `ACADEMIC_GRADED`, `ACADEMIC_EXCUSED` (excluded); `ACADEMIC_STATUS_UNKNOWN`, `ACADEMIC_STATUS_INCONSISTENT` (asked, not scheduled); `ACADEMIC_NOT_DUE_SOON`, `ACADEMIC_DUE_UNKNOWN` (deferred); `ACADEMIC_OVERDUE`, `ACADEMIC_CRITICAL`; `ACADEMIC_EFFORT_UNKNOWN` (provisional allocation); `ACADEMIC_READY_TO_SUBMIT`; coverage risks `ACADEMIC_UNCOVERED`, `ACADEMIC_PARTIAL`. Urgent work that cannot be fully scheduled: `CRITICAL_TASK_UNSCHEDULABLE` (nothing fits), `CRITICAL_TASK_PARTIAL`, with `cause` `NO_TIME_BEFORE_DEADLINE`, `NOT_ENOUGH_FREE_TIME` or `DAILY_STUDY_CAP`, and the matching warning `CRITICAL_ACADEMIC_CONFLICT`.

## Options rejected (never shown as plans)
`USER_REJECTED`, `REPORTED_UNAVAILABLE`, `PREFERENCE_AVOIDED`, `MISSING_SOURCE_URL`, `MISSING_CHECKED_AT`, `EVENT_CANCELED`, `EVENT_POSTPONED`, `EVENT_ALREADY_ENDED`, `SOLD_OUT`, `INVALID_TIME_RANGE`, `INVALID_COST`, `COST_EXCEEDS_BUDGET`, `OUTSIDE_WINDOW`, `ACTIVITY_CONFLICTS_COMMITMENT`, `TRAVEL_EXCEEDS_AVAILABLE_TIME`, `TRANSITION_BUFFER_TOO_TIGHT`, `ACTIVITY_CLOSED_DURING_WINDOW`, `NO_FEASIBLE_SLOT`.

## Options provisional (may appear in a plan, labeled)
`STALE_CHECK`, `CHECKED_AT_IN_FUTURE`, `CHANGES_REPORTED`, `REGISTRATION_REQUIRED`, `ONLY_AGGREGATOR_SOURCE`, `TIME_UNVERIFIED`, `PRICE_UNVERIFIED`, `AVAILABILITY_UNVERIFIED`, `AVAILABILITY_UNKNOWN`, `HOURS_UNKNOWN`, `HOURS_UNVERIFIED`, `COST_UNKNOWN`, `LOCATION_UNKNOWN`.

Provisional and not placeable (listed under `options`, never on a timeline): `DURATION_UNKNOWN`, `END_TIME_UNKNOWN`, `TRAVEL_UNKNOWN`.

Plan-level: `TRAVEL_ASSUMED`, `COST_INCOMPLETE`, `AVAILABILITY_NOT_VERIFIED`, `COMMITMENT_MARKED_FREE` (ignored_commitments), `COMBINED_COST_EXCEEDS_BUDGET` (diagnostics), `NO_FREE_TIME`, `EMPTY_DAY` (status `empty_day`, not an error), `NO_FEASIBLE_COMBINATION`.
