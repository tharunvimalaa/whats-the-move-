Got assignments due but still tryna have a life? What's the Move? has you covered.

1. Add it to Cubbie

Send Cubbie this link:

https://github.com/tharunvimalaa/whats-the-move-/tree/main/whats-the-move

Approve the installation, and you're good.

2. Connect your life

Make sure your Google Calendar and Canvas are connected to Cubbie. That way, it can check your actual schedule, assignments, deadlines, and commitments instead of guessing.

3. Just ask what's the move

Literally text Cubbie something like:

"Yo, what's the move tomorrow? Got like $30, need to lock in on some homework, but I wanna do something fun too."

4. Let it cook 🍳

What's the Move checks your availability, figures out what schoolwork needs attention, and looks for real things happening around you.

You'll get a personalized plan that balances getting your work done with actually enjoying your day.

5. Your day, your call

Want more studying? More going out? A cheaper plan? Just tell Cubbie. It can help you explore different options without changing your calendar unless you approve.

No fake events. No random made-up plans. Unverified details and estimates are labeled.
