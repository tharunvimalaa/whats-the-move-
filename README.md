# What's the Move?

A Cubbie Hackathon **Track 1** Skill (Milestone 1). It helps a college student plan a realistic day around unfinished academic work, classes, a job, free time, a spending limit and real things to do near campus, and returns a timed schedule rather than a list of ideas. First test campus: Arizona State University, Tempe. Nothing in the engine is campus-specific. sign into your canvas and google account with cubbie to plan your day
